# Unmask journal quiz images

These three AI-generated illustrations explain a self-observation question, not a diagnosis or an expected appearance. Generated with the built-in image-generation tool, reviewed visually, then converted to WebP for the app. The original generated PNGs are retained outside the app source.

Shared prompt: A premium editorial illustrated portrait for the Unmask skincare self-observation quiz. A clearly visible adult Black Nigerian woman, natural normal skin texture, clean warm bathroom or mirror setting, off-white and sage background with wine accents. Centered landscape composition, no text, branding, watermark, before/after promises or exaggerated skin conditions.

| Asset | Question | Scene prompt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| cleansing.webp | After gentle cleansing, how does your skin usually feel? | A woman gently touching her cheek after washing, beside a mirror and folded towel. |
| shine.webp | By the afternoon, where do you usually notice shine? | A woman checking forehead shine with blotting paper and a hand mirror, forehead/nose subtly shiny and cheeks matte. |
| sensitivity.webp | Do skincare products often make your skin sting, burn or feel irritated? | A woman touching a mildly irritated cheek beside an unbranded skincare bottle, thoughtful expression, no injury or rash. |

General descriptors are informed by the [American Academy of Dermatology](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/skin-care-basics/care/skin-care-budget). The Unmask three-question flow is not a clinically validated test. It leaves conflicting or uncertain observations unclassified, treats sensitivity separately, and allows users to choose or change context manually. Photos are not used to diagnose or automatically score skin.
