# Brand Identity

## Name
**Sourced** (working name — pending trademark/domain verification, which needs an actual USPTO/trademark search and domain check before committing).

Why: a real, plain word — easy to say, spell, remember. Encodes the actual differentiator (real people's sourced experience, not AI-generated or brand-asserted claims). Doesn't lock the brand to skincare, so it still works when expanding to other categories later.

**Backups if unavailable:** Verafy (verify + affirm), Provent (proven + event). Avoided: Clarive (too close to Clarivate, an established IP-analytics company).

---

## Color Palette

| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Shell | `#FBF5F1` | Background |
| Ink | `#2A211D` | Body text (warm charcoal, not pure black) |
| Rosewood | `#8C3547` | Primary brand color — CTAs, active states, wordmark |
| Honey | `#D9A441` | Secondary accent — badges, highlights |
| Sage | `#6E8F73` | Positive signals — streaks, completed routine, strong match |
| Mist | `#E7DED7` | Borders, dividers, card backgrounds |

**Why these choices:** Deliberately avoids the most common AI-generated design defaults — the warm-cream + terracotta-serif combo, near-black + neon accent themes, and generic SaaS card-with-shadow kits. Rosewood reads as warm and confident without defaulting to orange-terracotta, and works well across the diverse skin tones featured throughout the product's photography and community content. No clinical pharma blue/mint, no pastel-pink skincare cliché.

---

## Typography

**Single family: General Sans** (free, via Fontshare) — humanist, rounded terminals, reads warm rather than corporate at small mobile sizes. Use weight range (Light/Regular/Medium/Semibold/Bold) to carry the full hierarchy rather than introducing a second typeface.

A custom lettering treatment is reserved for the wordmark/logo only — that's where the brand gets a distinctive signature mark, not through a serif-everywhere habit across the interface.

---

## Icon Style

- Line icons, consistent 1.5–2px stroke, rounded caps and joins (slightly organic, not perfectly geometric)
- Base library: **Phosphor Icons** (open-source, wide style range, easy to theme)
- Pattern: outline for inactive states, filled + Rosewood for active/selected states (nav, favorited items)
- Avoid: gradients, 3D/skeuomorphic effects, generic shield icon for Confidence Score (overused security/fintech cliché — use a soft circular badge motif instead, since the score is about fit, not security)

---

## Voice & Tone (carries through UI copy)

- Plain, direct, active voice — "Save changes," not "Submit"
- Never sells or oversells — describes what something is/does, doesn't hype it
- Errors and empty states explain what happened and what to do next, in the interface's voice — never guilt-based, never vague
- No exclamation-heavy enthusiasm, no manufactured urgency language — consistent with the Guiding Principles
